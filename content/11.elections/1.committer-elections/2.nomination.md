title: "What Should a Nomination Look Like?"
description: "A committer nomination should explain the candidate’s contributions to the project and thus why they should be elected as a committer."
---

A committer nomination should explain the candidate’s contributions to the project and thus why they should be elected as a committer. Cite the issues they have fixed via patches; cite the community forum postings they have answered; cite the [dev-list](/project-resources-and-services/mailing-lists/#project-dev-lists) design discussions to which they have contributed; etc. In all cases, provide URLs to source material.
