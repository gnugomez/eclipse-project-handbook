[Project Management Committee](/project-roles/project-management-committee/) (PMC) members are elected by the existing PMC leads and members, and approved by the EMO/ED. The criteria by which a PMC *Member* is selected varies by PMC. Some PMCs are set up to have a representative from each of the projects in the top-level project. Other PMCs are more exclusive and require a demonstration of merit.

In all cases, the PMC Lead makes a recommendation to the EMO/ED to appoint a PMC Member. The final decision rests with the EMO/ED.

> **Tip:** No specific infrastructure is exists for PMC Member elections. PMCs must use their PMC mailing list to nominate and elect new members, and report the results to the [EMO](mailto:emo@eclipse-foundation.org). The EMO will get approval from the EMO/ED and make the appointment.
