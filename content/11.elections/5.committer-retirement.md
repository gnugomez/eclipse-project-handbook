---
title: "Committer Retirement"
description: "A committer can retire themselves from a project."
---

A committer can retire themselves from a project. Retirement cannot be undone: to rejoin a project as a committer after being retired, a contributor must again be [nominated and elected](/elections/committer-elections/) into the role.

> **Tip:** To retire from a project, navigate to the [project page](/project-management-infrastructure/project-pages/), open the *Who’s Involved* tab, and click on your image in the committer list. Then, in the "Retire Committer Status" box on the on the project role page, check the checkbox and click the **Submit** button.
>
> ::media{size="natural"}
> ![elections retire self](/images/elections/elections-retire-self.png)
> ::

A project lead may also retire a committer. A project lead might, for example, retire a committer that has been inactive for an extended period of time (e.g., longer than a year). Before retiring a committer, the project’s community should be informed of the change and the committer must be given a chance to defend retaining their status via the project’s [dev-list](/project-resources-and-services/mailing-lists/#project-dev-lists).

Retired committers are listed as *Historic Committers* on a project’s *Who’s Involved* page. To restore an historic committer to committer status, they must be [re-elected](/elections/committer-elections/) into the role.

> **Note:** A common practice is for the project lead to use the project’s [dev-list](/project-resources-and-services/mailing-lists/#project-dev-lists) to announce generically that they’re going to retire committers that have been inactive for some designated period of time and give the committers a chance to speak up before a deadline.

There is no means by which a committer can be forced out of a project if they have not done anything wrong (there is no notion of "voting a committer out" of a project). Leaving the company that sponsors the project, for example, is not a valid reason for retirement. On the other hand, that a committer has changed jobs or roles and can no longer continue to contribute the project is a completely valid reason for retirement.
