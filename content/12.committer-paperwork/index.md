The Eclipse Foundation needs to ensure that all [committers](/project-roles/committers/) with write access to the code, websites, and issue tracking systems understand their role in the intellectual property process. The Eclipse Foundation also needs to ensure that we have accurate records of the people who are acting as change agents on the projects. To ensure that committers understand their role, and that the Eclipse Foundation has accurate records, committers must provide documentation asserting that they have read, understood, and will follow the committer guidelines.

## Committer Questionnaire

When you are granted committer status (either by [election](/elections/committer-elections/) or appointment), you will receive an email from our automated process asking you to complete the *Committer Questionnaire*. This form’s workflow determines which agreements we need from you.

::media{size="natural"}
![committer questionnaire](/images/committer-paperwork/committer-questionnaire.png)

#caption
The Committer Agreement Workflow starts by asking for employment status.
::

If you indicate that you are employed, you will be asked to identify your employer. Only member companies that have provided an [MCCA](/committer-paperwork/#member-committer-and-contributor-agreement) will be listed as member companies in the *Committer Questionnaire*. If you work for a company that is not an Eclipse Foundation Member Company, or your employer has not provided the Eclipse Foundation with a *MCCA* you must list your employer as "Other".

> **Warning:** Whether or not you have the necessary authority to sign these forms is a matter between you and your employer. Check with your manager.

The *Committer Questionnaire* is accessible only after you have been granted the role of committer on a project (note that all committer relationships are between an individual and a specific project). You will not be granted committer privileges until after we have the necessary agreements in place.

> **Note:** Committer documents are all handled electronically. *Do not send signed documents via email unless the EMO Records Team asks you to do so.*
>
> The Eclipse Foundation uses Dropbox Sign to handle electronic documents. Our automated process will contact you via email to notify you when an electronic document needs to be signed. You will also receive separate email from Dropbox Sign with information regarding the specific forms that require your signature. If you do not receive an email within a few hours of a successful committer election, please check your spam folder.
>
> If you have questions or concerns, contact the [EMO Records Team](mailto:emo-records@eclipse-foundation.org).

## Documents

The specific agreements required depends on the nature of the project and the employment status of the individual. For committers on an open source *software* project (that is, most Eclipse projects), the agreements described below are required.

For committers on an open source *specification* project, additional [working group agreements](/specifications/committers/#specification-development-agreements) are required.

After all required documents have been received, the EMO Records Team will review them, contact the committer to resolve any issues that need to be addressed, and send a request to the Eclipse Infrastructure Team to provision a committer account.

Questions regarding this process should be addressed to the [EMO Records Team](mailto:emo-records@eclipse-foundation.org).

### Member Committer and Contributor Agreement

The [Member Committer and Contributor Agreement](https://www.eclipse.org/legal/committer_process/EclipseMemberCommitterAgreementFinal.pdf) (MCCA) is used by organisations that are members of the Eclipse Foundation to cover all of their employees who participate in Eclipse Foundation projects as committers.

> **Note:** If your employer is a member of the Eclipse Foundation and has not already provided an MCCA, consult with your management team to determine who has the necessary authority to sign it on your company’s behalf. Provide the MCCA in advance of the completion of your committer election or new project creation to streamline the committer provisioning process. If you and your management team are not sure whether or not your employer has an MCCA, ask the [EMO Records Team](mailto:emo-records@eclipse-foundation.org).

If the committer’s employer is a member of the Eclipse Foundation that cannot provide a signed MCCA, then the committer will have to complete an *Individual Committer Agreement*.

### Individual Committer Agreement

The [Individual Committer Agreement](https://www.eclipse.org/legal/committer_process/EclipseIndividualCommitterAgreementFinal.pdf) (ICA) is used by committers who are not covered by a [MCCA](https://www.eclipse.org/legal/committer_process/EclipseMemberCommitterAgreementFinal.pdf).

> **Note:** The link to the Individual Committer Agreement is provided for **informational purposes only**. All committer forms are managed electronically. The EMO Records Team will contact you if we need you to sign the ICA.

An ICA is required when:

- The committer works for a company that is not a member of the Eclipse Foundation;

- The committer works for member company that has not signed a MCCA;

- The committer is self employed or not employed; or

- The committer is a student.

### Existing Committer

If the individual is already a committer on an existing Eclipse Foundation project then additional agreements may or may not be necessary. The EMO Records Team will ask for additional documentation if required.

### Not Employed or Student

If the committer is not employed or is a student, they must send a note to the [EMO Records Team](mailto:emo-records@eclipse-foundation.org) explaining their status.

## Frequently Asked Questions

::accordion{multiple}
  :::accordion-item
  #title
  What is the difference between an Individual Committer Agreement, the Member Committer and Contributor Agreement, and the Eclipse Contributor Agreement?

  #default
  The [Individual Committer Agreement](/committer-paperwork/#individual-committer-agreement) (ICA) is for committers whose employers are either not [members](https://www.eclipse.org/membership/) of the Eclipse Foundation or are members that have not signed the Member Committer and Contributor Agreement.

  The [Member Committer and Contributor Agreement](/committer-paperwork/#member-committer-and-contributor-agreement) (MCCA) is used by organisations that are members of the Eclipse Foundation to cover all of their employees who participate in Eclipse Foundation open source projects as committers.

  The [Eclipse Contributor Agreement](/contributing/eclipse-contributor-agreement/) (ECA) is for everybody else. The ECA covers contributors who are not otherwise covered for their contributions by an ICA or MCCA.
  :::

  :::accordion-item
  #title
  What happens if I do not fill out the agreements?

  #default
  If you do not fill out the agreements, then you do not get your credentials for write-access to the source code repository(s) and other project resources.
  :::

  :::accordion-item
  #title
  Where can I get help to discuss these documents with my management team?

  #default
  The EMO can talk to your management and senior executives about these (and other) legal documents to help them understand why these documents are the best risk reduction solution for everyone involved. Contact us at <license@eclipse-foundation.org>.
  :::

  :::accordion-item
  #title
  What formats can be used to submit paper documentation?

  #default
  The EMO Records Team prefers a scanned document delivered via email. The documents may also be provided by fax or by post (the fax number and postal address are captured in the documents).
  :::

  :::accordion-item
  #title
  What Email address should I use to send completed documents?

  #default
  <emo-records@eclipse-foundation.org>
  :::

  :::accordion-item
  #title
  What if a I change employers?

  #default
  Edit your [Eclipse Account Profile](https://accounts.eclipse.org/) to update your employment status.
  :::
::
