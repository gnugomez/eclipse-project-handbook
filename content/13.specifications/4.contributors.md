---
title: "Contributors"
description: "Anybody that contributes to an Eclipse specification project is a contributor."
---

Anybody that contributes to an Eclipse specification project is a contributor. Contributors make pull requests against project repositories. Pull requests must be reviewed and merged by a committer.

::media{size="natural"}
![specification contributors](/images/specifications/specification-contributors.svg){width="259"}

#caption
How to become a contributor
::

To become a contributor, an individual must:

1.  Create an [Eclipse Foundation account](/contributing/eclipse-foundation-account/);

2.  Sign the [Eclipse Contributor Agreement](/contributing/eclipse-contributor-agreement/) (ECA);

3.  Contribute! (submit a pull request)

> **Note:** If you are already a committer, your committer agreement includes an ECA; so you’re already covered. If your employer is a member of the Eclipse Foundation and your employer has signed the [Member Committer and Contributor Agreement](/committer-paperwork/#member-committer-and-contributor-agreement) (MCCA), then you’re already covered. You can check your status on the [Eclipse Foundation account](/contributing/eclipse-foundation-account/) page (your ECA status is in the top-right corner). If you’re not sure contact the [EMO Records Team](mailto:emo-records@eclipse-foundation.org) for assistance.

Regular contributors of high quality content should be invited to join the specification project team as a committer (via [committer election](/elections/committer-elections/)).
