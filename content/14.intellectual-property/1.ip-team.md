The Eclipse Foundation has a well-defined [IP Policy](https://eclipse.org/org/documents/Eclipse_IP_Policy.pdf), corresponding IP Due Diligence Process, and an *IP Team* of dedicated professional IP specialists who perform the heavy lifting in the due diligence process.

[Committers](/project-roles/committers/), the software developers who decide what will become *project content* and how an Eclipse open source project will leverage *third party content*, are responsible for bringing IP issues to the attention of the IP Team.
