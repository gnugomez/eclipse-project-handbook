Initiating a termination review is a crucial step in managing projects that have reached the end of their life cycle or are no longer sustainable. The EMO will initiate a termination review when requested by the PMC or a project lead, or when the EMO suspects that the project is no longer viable.

As part of a termination review, the EMO will (with assistance from the PMC and project team) provide a rationale for the termination, outlining the reasons behind it, such as lack of activity or relevance, and the potential implications for the Eclipse community. The EMO schedules a formal review period that will run for at least a week, during which the Eclipse community can provide feedback and comments on the termination.

> **Note:** Send an email to [EMO](mailto:emo@eclipse-foundation.org) to initiate a termination review.

During the termination review period the Eclipse community engages in a thorough evaluation, providing feedback and participating in relevant discussions related to the impact of the termination. During this period, there is always the possibility that someone may express interest in taking over the project. In such cases, the termination review is withdrawn, and instead, a restructuring or progress review is scheduled, depending on the specific circumstances.

The EMO uses this [GitLab template](https://gitlab.eclipse.org/eclipsefdn/emo-team/emo/-/blob/main/.gitlab/issue_templates/project_termination.md?ref_type=heads/) as a starting point to track the status of termination reviews. The template is always modified to align with the specific termination rationale.
