[GitLab](/project-resources-and-services/gitlab/) groups, [GitHub](/project-resources-and-services/github/) organisations, and other source repositories are listed on the *Developer Resources* page.

Metadata regarding source repositories is maintained by EMO staff.

A *Contribution Message*, which is — when provided — displayed at the top of the *Developer Resources* page. Arbitrary text is permitted, but we recommend that you limit this content to a single paragraph with a few sentences that include a link to more information.

> **Tip:** It is more likely that potential contributors will find your project’s source code repositories before they find the project page; be sure to provide a [Contributor Guide](/legal-documentation/contributor-guide/) in the root of your project repositories.

To edit information about contribution and provide pointers to information about contributing to your project:

- Log into the PMI;

- Navigate to the [project page](/project-management-infrastructure/project-pages/);

- Click **Edit**;

- Open the section labelled *Source Code*;

- Update the fields as required;

- Click **Save**.
