title: "Company Logos"
description: "Company logos automatically appear on the Overview and Who’s Involved page under the following conditions:"
---

Company logos automatically appear on the *Overview* and *Who’s Involved* page under the following conditions:

- The company must be a [member](https://www.eclipse.org/membership/) of the Eclipse Foundation;

- The company needs to have their logo uploaded to the [Eclipse Foundation Member Portal](https://membership.eclipse.org/portal); and

- An employee of the company has authored a commit in the last three months that’s been merged into a project repository.

> **Tip:** The display of company logos is *entirely automatic* based on the criteria listed above.

## Troubleshooting

- **A company employee must have a recent commit**

  Has an employee authored a commit within the last three months?

  Note that this computation is based on commit dates; it does not consider merge or pull requests, only commits.

- **The mapping between a commit and company uses the commit author’s email address**

  Does the email that the employee uses for the *Author* field in the commit record match the email address that they’ve used for their [Eclipse Foundation Account](/contributing/eclipse-foundation-account/)?

  If not, the employee must either change their email address in their account, or start using their Eclipse Foundation account email address for their Git credentials.

- **The employee must be associated with their member company**

  Does the employee appear in the member portal?

  If not, there are two solutions: the company’s member representative may reach out to [EMO Records](mailto:emo-records@eclipse-foundation.org) to add the employee; or the employee may [align](/contributing/eclipse-foundation-account/#employed-by-a-member-company) their Eclipse Foundation account with the member company.

If the logo is still not showing up, [create a Help Desk Issue](https://gitlab.eclipse.org/eclipsefdn/helpdesk) to request assistance.
