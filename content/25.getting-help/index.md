If you have any questions, or are unsure of your responsibilities as a [project lead](/project-roles/project-lead/) or [committer](/project-roles/committers/), please contact your [Project Management Committee](/project-roles/project-management-committee/) (PMC), or the [Eclipse Management Organization](/project-roles/eclipse-management-organization/) (EMO) at <emo@eclipse-foundation.org>.

To request assistance with matters related to supported project infrastructure (e.g., Git repositories, mailing lists, builds), open a [Help Desk](https://gitlab.eclipse.org/eclipsefdn/helpdesk) issue.

To report vulnerabilities or report security issues contact <security@eclipse-foundation.org>. Please see [Managing and Reporting Vulnerabilities](/vulnerabilities/).

To ask questions about licensing or issues of a legal nature, contact <license@eclipse-foundation.org>.

If you’re not sure, contact <emo@eclipse-foundation.org>.
