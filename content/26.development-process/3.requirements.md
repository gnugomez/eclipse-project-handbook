This document is entirely composed of requirements. In addition to the requirements specified in this development process, the EMO is instructed to clarify, expand, and extend this process by creating a set of development guidelines to advance the creation, evolution, promotion, and support of the open source Projects; and to cultivate both a community and an Ecosystem of complementary products and services.

## Eclipse Foundation Responsibility

The Eclipse Foundation acts as the open source steward for all Eclipse Projects by providing the legal framework, infrastructure, and governance required to ensure project technical integrity and regulatory compliance while facilitating transparent, vendor-neutral collaboration.

## EMO Responsibility

The EMO has the responsibility and authority to mitigate issues that arise when Committers fail to perform the required behaviours or engage in practices that risk harm to Eclipse Projects, the community, and/or Ecosystem. This includes, but is not limited to, issues that arise due to a failure to implement the [Eclipse Foundation Security Policy](https://www.eclipse.org/security/policy/), the [Eclipse Foundation Intellectual Property Policy](https://eclipse.org/org/documents/Eclipse_IP_Policy.pdf), the [Eclipse Foundation Community Code of Conduct](https://www.eclipse.org/org/documents/community-code-of-conduct), or other governance policies of the Eclipse Foundation.

The EMO’s authority includes, but is not limited to, the ability to grant specific individuals equivalent to Committer privileges, suspend access to Project resources, add or remove Committers and Project Leads, and — in extreme cases — terminate the Project.

The EMO is not permitted to override or ignore the requirements listed in this document without the express written endorsement of the Board of Directors.

The motivation for the EMO to take action along with description of the steps taken as part of the mitigation must be publicly disclosed within one week of action being taken. The [Grievance Handling](/development-process/grievance-handling/) section of this document describes the means by which a Member may respond in the event that they believe that the EMO has overstepped its authority.
