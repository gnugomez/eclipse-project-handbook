---

Projects are the level of communication with the larger Eclipse community and Ecosystem. Projects may either have their own communication channels or they may be part of a parent Project’s communication channels. In either case, the Project is required to maintain an open and public communication channel with the Eclipse community including, but not limited to, Project plans, schedules, and design discussions.

All Projects must make the communication channels easy to find. Projects are further required to make the separate communication channels of their child Projects (if any) easy to find.
