---
title: "Releases"
description: "Any Project, with exception of Permanent Incubators, may make a Release."
---

Any Project, with exception of Permanent Incubators, may make a Release. A Release may include the code from any subset of the Project’s descendants.

Releases are, by definition, anything that is distributed outside of the Committers of a Project. If consumers are being directed to download a build, then that build has been released. All Projects and Committers must obey the Eclipse Foundation requirements on approving any Release.

The [Eclipse IP Policy](https://eclipse.org/org/documents/Eclipse_IP_Policy.pdf) must be followed at all times. Prior to issuing any Release, the Project Lead must assert that the IP Policy has been followed and all approvals have been received.

A Project can make Releases for one calendar year following a successful [Progress Review](/development-process/process/reviews/#progress-review) or [Release Review](/development-process/process/reviews/#release-review). The Project Leadership Chain may — at its discretion — require that the Project engage in additional Reviews (e.g. a progress or Release Review) prior to issuing a Release.

Releases and corresponding artifacts for Projects in the Incubation Phase must be labelled to indicate the incubation status of the Project.
