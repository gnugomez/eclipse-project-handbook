The [incubation mailing list](https://dev.eclipse.org/mailman/listinfo/incubation) exists to facilitate discussions related to the incubation phase, allowing project committers to ask questions about Eclipse Foundation processes, seek guidance from the [Eclipse Technical Advisory Council](/project-roles/eclipse-technical-advisory-council/), [Eclipse Management Organization](/project-roles/eclipse-management-organization/), and connect with other community members involved in projects in the incubation phase.

New committers are encouraged to join the incubation mailing list; this list is a good place to ask questions about process, services available, and other aspects of working as a committer.
