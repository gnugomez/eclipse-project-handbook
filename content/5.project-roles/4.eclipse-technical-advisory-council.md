The Eclipse Technical Advisory Council (ETAC) serves the community by identifying and tackling any issues that hinder Eclipse’s continued technological success and innovation, widespread adoption, and future growth. The Eclipse Technical Advisory Council is responsible for guiding and influencing governance structures and advises the EMO and executive director in matters of importance to developers (including, but not limited to, proposing changes to the [Eclipse Foundation Development Process](/development-process/) (EDP)).

> **Note:** The Eclipse Technical Advisory Council had previously been referred to as the Eclipse *Architecture* Council (with slightly modified responsibilities).

Comprising technical leaders from Eclipse open source projects and the community, it is the council’s goal to keep the projects successful and healthy, the processes simple and smooth, and the communities vibrant and cohesive.

More concretely, the Eclipse Technical Advisory Council serves three basic purposes:

- To maintain and interpret the [Eclipse Foundation Development Process](/development-process/);

- To provide advice to the [Eclipse Management Organization](/project-roles/eclipse-management-organization/);and

- To contribute best practices and advice to Eclipse open source projects.

Every [PMC](/project-roles/project-management-committee/) may appoint a representative to the Technical Advisory Council to represent the interests of the PMC and corresponding Top-level Project. Every Strategic Member of the Eclipse Foundation may also appoint a representative. Other Technical Advisory Council Members are nominated and voted on by the Technical Advisory Council itself, and appointed to the council by the Eclipse Foundation’s Executive Director. Appointed members serve a two year renewable term.

The Technical Advisory Council interacts primarily via their [mailing list](https://accounts.eclipse.org/mailing-list/eclipse.org-architecture-council). List participation is limited only to Technical Advisory Council members and subscription to the list is automatic. Additionally, the Technical Advisory Council meets (remotely) every month for a one-hour closed discussion (with public minutes).

[Committers](/project-roles/committers/) and [Project Leads](/project-roles/project-lead/) should connect with the Technical Advisory Council via their PMC.
