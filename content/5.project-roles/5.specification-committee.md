Specification committees are a feature of [working groups](https://www.eclipse.org/org/workinggroups/) and operate under the terms defined in the corresponding working group’s charter.

A specification committee plays an important role in the governance of [specification projects](/specifications/) that operate under their purview according to the [Eclipse Foundation Specification Process](https://www.eclipse.org/projects/efsp/) (EFSP). For completeness, specification committees have *no role* in the governance of *regular* (technology) open source projects (that is, projects that are not engaged in the process of creating specifications).

> **Tip:** For more information about specification committees, please see [Specification Committee](/specifications/life-cycle/#specification-committee) in the Specifications section of this document.
