The Eclipse Management Organization (EMO) consists of the Eclipse Foundation staff, and the Eclipse Technical Advisory Council. The EMO is responsible for providing services to the projects, facilitating project reviews, resolving issues, and more. The EMO is the maintainer of the Eclipse Foundation Development Process. The best method of contact with the EMO is by email (<emo@eclipse-foundation.org>). If you have a question that cannot be answered by project lead, or [PMC](/project-roles/project-management-committee/), ask the EMO.
