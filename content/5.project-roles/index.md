The [Eclipse Foundation Development Process](/development-process/) defines multiple formal open source project roles. Primary among the roles are those of [Committer](/project-roles/committers/), [Project Lead](/project-roles/project-lead/), and [Project Management Committee](/project-roles/project-management-committee/).
