- **Generate a correct SBOM in a standard format for products**

  This includes both the files that are contained within the product along with third party content embedded or referenced by the product. As of this writing, there are two popular standard formats: [SPDX](https://spdx.dev/) and [CycloneDX](https://cyclonedx.org/).

- **Must be able to resolve entries to specific content via unambiguous and durable coordinates**

  The [Package URL](https://github.com/package-url/purl-spec) (pURL) format (e.g, `pkg:maven/org.glassfish.jersey.inject/jersey-hk2@2.35`) to specify the namespace, name, and version of a library.

- **Correctly identify the license for each entry**

  For project content (that is, files) this is the license information for the specific file. For dependencies (whether they are packaged in the product or are referenced/required by it), this is the license of the entire component.

- **Distribute SBOMs with artifacts**

  Upload SBOMs along with the artifacts they represent. SBOMs should be, for example, uploaded alongside other Maven artifacts.

- **Be a good open source citizen**

  As good open source citizens, we have a responsibility to make it as easy as possible for others to generate SBOMs using our content. To that end, whether or not it makes sense to create an SBOM for a specific project artifact, we should provide consistent [copyright headers](/legal-documentation/copyright-headers/) that correctly express the copyright and licence (using `SPDX-License-Identifier`) and the most complete metadata (e.g., [Maven metadata](/software-bill-of-materials/maven-based-java-projects/#maven-metadata-best-practices)) possible to ensure that adopters are able to create the most accurate SBOMs possible.
