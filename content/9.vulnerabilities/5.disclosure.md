Disclosure is initially limited to the reporter, the project team and the Eclipse Foundation Security Team, but is expanded to include other individuals, and the general public. The timing and manner of disclosure is governed by the [Eclipse Foundation Security Policy](https://www.eclipse.org/security/policy/).

Publicly disclosed issues are listed on the [Known Eclipse Security Vulnerabilities](https://www.eclipse.org/security/known.php) page.
